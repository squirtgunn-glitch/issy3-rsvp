Issaquah 3rd
Reunion Weekend

July 17–19, 2026  ·  Wheeler Residence  ·  Woodinville, WA
// EVENT INFO //

It's been 30 years since many of us roamed the halls of Issaquah High, woke up early for seminary shenanigans, or raced on the wet streets up the Plateau.

We'd like to invite you to a weekend of fun, memories and laughs as we get together to celebrate the moments, friends and people who shaped who we are today. The Wheelers have graciously offered their home as a gathering point and we'd love to spend as much time together as you can.

To help us prepare, please use the RSVP form to let us know what you are interested in. Once we have some numbers on interest we'll communicate out an agenda based on interest. We're thinking eventually we'll take this to a GroupMe to plan and prepare.

If you need help with somewhere to stay for a night or the weekend — please reach out to Anissa. They have lots of space and want everyone to be able to come who wants to regardless of circumstances.

We look forward to seeing you!
